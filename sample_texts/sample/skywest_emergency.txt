A SkyWest Airlines flight made an emergency landing in Buffalo, New York, on Wednesday after a passenger lost consciousness, officials said. The passenger received medical attention before being released, according to Marissa Snow, spokeswoman for SkyWest. She said the airline expects to accommodate the 75 passengers on another aircraft to their original destination - Hartford, Connecticut - later Wednesday afternoon. The Federal Aviation Administration initially reported a pressurization problem and said it would investigate, but later backed off that statement. SkyWest, a regional carrier, said there was a pressurization problem on the flight, which had originated in Chicago. "Following an emergency landing, paramedics met SkyWest Airlines flight 5622 because a passenger lost consciousness," the airline said. "The passenger received medical attention and the flight continued to Hartford." The aircraft, an Embraer E175, had 75 passengers and three crew members aboard. The plane descended 28,000 feet in three minutes. "It would feel like a roller coaster - when you go up and your stomach kind of upsets," a passenger said. The cause of the pressurization problem was under investigation, officials said.
